How to go live on each platform

Going live happens inside each platform's own app or studio, which is where your account and stream key stay safe. Tap a platform for the steps and a direct link.

Facebook
  1. Open the Facebook app, go to your ministry Page and tap Live video. On a computer, use Meta Business Suite or Live Producer.
  2. Write a clear title and description. Paste the post you wrote in the first tab.
  3. Run a short test with the audience set to Only me before the real service.
  4. Pin a comment with the prayer line and the giving details.
Open Facebook
TikTok
  1. Open TikTok, tap the plus button and choose LIVE.
  2. TikTok decides who can go live, based on age and follower count. If LIVE does not appear, check your account's eligibility inside the app.
  3. Add a short title, such as "Deliverance and prayer service", and turn on comment moderation.
  4. TikTok favours portrait video, so hold the phone upright.
Open TikTok
YouTube
  1. Open YouTube Studio, choose Create, then Go live.
  2. The first time you enable live streaming, YouTube may take up to 24 hours to switch it on. Do this days before a big service.
  3. Set the title, thumbnail and a scheduled start time so followers can set a reminder.
Open YouTube Studio
All platforms at the same time
  • Use a computer with OBS Studio, plus a multistreaming service. Each platform gives you a stream URL and a stream key to paste in.
  • Treat each stream key like a password. Never post it or send it in a chat.
  • A phone alone goes live on one platform at a time. For several at once, use the computer route.
  • Use a wired connection or strong Wi-Fi. A steady upload speed of 5 Mbps or more suits 720p.
