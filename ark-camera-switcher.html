Cameras

Choose a device for each camera, then press Start. Click a picture or press keys 1 to 5 to put that camera on air.

How to use this to go live
  1. Press Allow cameras and microphone, then Start on each camera you have connected.
  2. Press Open clean output window. A second window opens showing only the on-air picture, with no buttons. Do not minimise it.
  3. In OBS Studio, add a Window Capture source and choose the window called "Ark of God Output". Add your microphone in OBS as well, because the output window is silent.
  4. Press Start Streaming in OBS to send it to Facebook, YouTube or TikTok. You can switch cameras from this page while streaming.
  5. No OBS? Some platforms and video-call tools let you share a window or screen. Share the output window the same way.
Tips and limits
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Keep this page and the output window visible, not minimised, or the picture can freeze.
  • Two or three USB cameras usually work. For more, use a powered USB hub or spread them across different ports. A camera can only be used by one app at a time, so close Zoom, OBS camera sources and other tabs first.
  • Recording keeps the video in the computer's memory until you press stop. For a service longer than about 90 minutes, record in OBS instead. The saved file is .webm and plays in Chrome or VLC.
  • Press Full screen to see the on-air picture alone. Press Escape to leave.
  • Everything runs on your computer. No video is uploaded anywhere by this page.
